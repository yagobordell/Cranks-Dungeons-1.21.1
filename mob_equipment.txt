================================================================================
CRANKS DUNGEONS - ACT 1 MOB EQUIPMENT & RUNIC GEAR SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

This document details the equipment, property generation, and runic stat rolling
system applied to humanoid mobs during ACT 1.

All equipment generated on mobs is dynamically baked via EquipmentStatApplier,
giving mobs real-time defensive attributes (Evasion, Energy Shield, Armor) and 
offensive bonuses (flat damage, elemental conversions, attack speed).

================================================================================
1. SPAWN CHANCES & PIECE DISTRIBUTION
================================================================================

Target Mobs:
- Zombies, Husks, Drowned, Zombie Villagers
- Skeletons, Strays, Bogged
- Piglins

Base Equipment Spawn Chance: ~22%
(78% of mobs spawn unarmored; 22% receive equipment from the Act 1 tables).

Piece Count Distribution:
- 1 Piece (Helmet, Boots, Chestplate, or Leggings): 60%
- 2 Pieces (e.g. Helmet + Boots, or Chestplate + Weapon): 25%
- Weapon Only (Sword, Bow, Crossbow): 10%
- Full 4-Piece Set (Elite mini-champion): 5%

Skeletons spawning with bows automatically have their bow processed through the
Act 1 Quality, Enchantment Slot, and Runic Affix pipeline.

================================================================================
2. ACT 1 ARMOR MATERIALS & ROLES
================================================================================

When armor is selected, materials are rolled according to Act 1 progression:

- LEATHER (40% Weight):
  Role: Evasive Skirmisher
  Stats: +60 Evasion Rating, +15% Movement Speed
  Behavior: Mobs dodge player physical attacks using the PoE 2 entropy dodge curve.

- GOLD (30% Weight):
  Role: Arcane / Shielded
  Stats: +35 Energy Shield, +55 Armor
  Behavior: Mobs spawn with an active Energy Shield that absorbs damage before health.

- CHAINMAIL (20% Weight):
  Role: Hybrid Armor / Evasion
  Stats: +60 Armor, +35 Evasion Rating, +30% Attack Speed

- IRON (10% Weight):
  Role: Heavy Physical Tank
  Stats: +120 Armor, +10 Toughness, -5% Movement Speed
  Behavior: High Physical Damage Reduction against incoming player hits.

- DIAMOND & NETHERITE (0% Weight):
  Gated until Act 2+ to prevent impossible early tanks and early-game loot trivialization.

================================================================================
3. ACT 1 WEAPONS (NO SPEARS)
================================================================================

When a weapon is equipped on a zombie, piglin, or skeleton:

- Wooden Sword: 25%
- Stone Sword: 45%
- Iron Sword: 25%
- Stone Shovel: 5%
- Bow / Crossbow: Handled on ranged mobs (Skeletons/Strays/Piglins)

Spears are explicitly disabled from Act 1 mob tables for now.

================================================================================
4. QUALITY ROLLING CURVE (EXPONENTIAL DECAY)
================================================================================

Every item piece has a 10% base chance to roll bonus Quality (1% to 20%).
Quality scales the item's baseline stats (Armor, Evasion, ES, Attack Damage).

Higher quality values are significantly rarer following a steep exponential curve:

- 1% to 5% Quality:   55% chance (Common Quality)
- 6% to 10% Quality:  25% chance (Good Quality)
- 11% to 15% Quality: 12% chance (High Quality)
- 16% to 19% Quality: 6% chance  (Exceptional Quality)
- 20% Quality (Max):  2% chance  (Perfect Quality Jackpot ~0.2% overall)

================================================================================
5. ENCHANTMENT SLOTS PROGRESSION
================================================================================

Items on mobs have a small chance to spawn with pre-unlocked Enchantment Slots
(obeying the item's maximum slot capacity via EnchantmentSlotHelper):

- 1 Slot:  5.0% chance on eligible items
- 2 Slots: 0.5% chance (Only on Chestplates, Leggings, Bows/Crossbows)
- 3 Slots: 0.05% chance (Only on Tridents)

Act 1 Rule: Items spawn with 0 actual enchantments, leaving the unlocked slots
clean and ready for player enchanting via the Anvil / Enchanting Table.

================================================================================
6. RUNIC AFFIX SYSTEM (TIERS 10 TO 8)
================================================================================

Items roll their Runic Rarity:

- NORMAL (0 Runes): 70%
- MAGIC (1-2 Runes): 24%
- RARE CHAMPION (3-4 Runes): 6%

Rune Tiers are strictly locked between Tier 10 and Tier 8 in Act 1:
- Tier 10 (Starter / Lowest):  60% of rune rolls
- Tier 9 (Early Mid Tier):    30% of rune rolls
- Tier 8 (Act 1 Ceiling):     10% of rune rolls
- Tiers 7 down to Tier 1:     0% (Locked until future Acts)

Affix Rules:
- Magic items: Up to 1 Prefix, 1 Suffix (Max 2 runes).
- Rare items: Up to 2 Prefixes, 2 Suffixes (Max 4 runes).
- Duplicate stat groups are strictly disallowed.

================================================================================
7. DROPS & PLAYER LOOTING
================================================================================

- Mobs equipped with Act 1 gear maintain the standard 8.5% drop chance.
- Slain mobs drop the EXACT rolled item with its Quality, open Enchantment Slots,
  and rolled Runic affixes preserved.
