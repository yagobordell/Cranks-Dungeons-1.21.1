================================================================================
CRANKS DUNGEONS - PROGRESSION & 12 COLOSSI SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW & PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The overarching objective of the game is to conquer "The End". However, reaching
and surviving in The End requires entering the true Endgame with high-tier gear,
capped resistances, and deep mechanical builds.

Vanilla Eyes of Ender CANNOT activate the End Portal. Instead, the 12 End Portal
frames require 12 Unique Colossus Eyes dropped by 12 ancient titans known as
"The Colossi".

CORE RULES:
- ONE-TIME SPAWN: Each Colossus exists only once per world. When defeated, it
  will NEVER spawn again.
- BIZARRE RITUAL SUMMONS: Colossi do not have crafting recipes. They require
  unusual, atmospheric, and ritualistic triggers (specific deaths, weather events,
  sacrifices, pocket nightmare dimensions).
- WORLD SHIFTS: Defeating key Colossi triggers permanent global world evolution
  (unlocking dimensions, scaling mob difficulty, unlocking rune tiers, or
  granting unique environmental buffs).
- 4-ACT SEPARATION: The 12 Colossi are divided across 4 Acts (3 Colossi per Act).
  Defeating the Capstone Colossus of an Act advances the world to the next Act.


================================================================================
2. THE 4 ACTS & LEVEL PROGRESSION
================================================================================

ACT 1: THE AWAKENING
- Environment: Overworld Surface & Shallow Caves (Y >= 0), Mineshafts
- Level & Gear: Level 1-15 | 50-150 HP | Tier 1-3 Runes (Leather/Iron tier)
- Theme: Basic physical combat, introducing small Evasion and basic Armor.
- Loot Pool: Tome of Transmutation, Armourer's Scrap, Blacksmith's Whetstone.
- Capstone: Sylvanus, the Hollow Warden (Colossus 3).

ACT 2: THE DEPTHS & RUIN
- Environment: Deepslate Caves (Y < 0), Trial Chambers, Ocean Monuments, Raids
- Level & Gear: Level 16-30 | 150-350 HP | Tier 4-6 Runes (Diamond tier)
- Theme: Elemental threats begin (Freeze buildup, Lightning Thorns, Energy Shield).
- Loot Pool: Tome of Augmentation, Regal Tome, Tome of Alchemy, Deepslate Sigil.
- Capstone: Ignis, Lord of the Molten Core (Colossus 6).

ACT 3: THE INFERNAL CRUCIBLE
- Environment: The Nether Dimension (Fortresses, Bastions, Nether Wastes)
- Level & Gear: Level 31-45 | 350-550 HP | Tier 7-8 Runes (Netherite tier)
- Theme: Mandatory Fire Resistance, Void conversions, heavy Armor Penetration.
- Loot Pool: Exalted Tome, Tome of Chaos, Tome of Annulment, Divine Tome.
- Capstone: Pyroth, the Blazing Sovereign (Colossus 9).

ACT 4: THE VOID FRONTIER
- Environment: Ancient Cities, High Peaks, Stronghold, The End Dimension
- Level & Gear: Level 46-60+ | 550-800+ HP | Tier 9-10 Perfect Runes (Endgame)
- Theme: Massive Void damage, heavy Penetration, pure build test.
- Loot Pool: Perfect Exalted Tomes, Perfect Tomes of Chaos, Artificial Tomes.
- Capstone / Final: Ouroboros, Keeper of the Threshold (Colossus 12).


================================================================================
3. THE 12 COLOSSI - DETAILED ENCOUNTERS & WORLD SHIFTS
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COLOSSUS 1: Ymir, the Frostborn Revenant (Act 1)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Location: Snowy Biomes / Frozen Peaks
- Spawn Ritual: Die to freezing / powder snow during a midnight blizzard. The player's
  corpse rises as Ymir, creating a localized permanent blizzard arena.
- Encounter: Frost aura that inflicts rapid Freeze Buildup. Ymir slams the ground
  with glacial pillars. Players must fight near heat sources (torches/campfires).
- Unique Drop: Frostbitten Eye + Rime-Forged Ring (Converts 25% Phys taken to Cold).
- World Shift ("The Great Thaw"):
  * Cold damage & freeze mechanics unlock globally.
  * Skeletons in snowy biomes permanently spawn as Strays with Act 1 cold scaling.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COLOSSUS 2: Thalassos, the Sunken Leviathan (Act 1)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Location: Deep Ocean Trenches
- Spawn Ritual: Drown in a Deep Ocean trench at midnight while carrying zero items.
  The player is dragged down into the pocket dimension: "The Sunken Abyss".
- Encounter (Time Attack):
  * 5 real-life minutes of mystical air before pressure crushes the player.
  * Dying inside respawns the player in the arena (timer keeps ticking).
  * Expiration ejections the player to the surface and resets the dimension.
- Unique Drop: Abyssal Eye + Tide-Caller's Conch (Permanent water breathing & +20% Lightning).
- World Shift ("The Oceanic Surge"):
  * Ocean trenches spawn luminous geysers.
  * Drowned gain +30% movement speed and 10% Life Leech.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COLOSSUS 3: Sylvanus, the Hollow Warden (Act 1 CAPSTONE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Location: Ancient Dark Forest
- Spawn Ritual: Locate an ancient Hollow Heartwood Tree and ignite it during a
  natural Thunderstorm. When lightning strikes the burning tree, Sylvanus emerges.
- Encounter: Root colosseum locking players in. Sweeping cleaves inflicting heavy Bleed.
- Unique Drop: Heartwood Eye + Deep Core Key (Unlocks Deepslate Dungeons).
- World Shift ("Earthquake & Act 2 Transition"):
  * Earth shatters: Overworld mobs level up to Act 2 stats (+80% HP, +50% Dmg).
  * Fissures open leading to Deepslate Dungeons (Y < 0).
  * Tier 4-6 Runes and Tomes of Augmentation begin dropping.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COLOSSUS 4: Arachna, Weaver of the Hollows (Act 2)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Location: Deepslate Caves (Y <= -40)
- Spawn Ritual: Step into a massive Cobweb Nest while suffering from Level 3+
  Poison and kill a spider with bare fists.
- Encounter: 3D vertical web arena. Arachna has 85% Evasion Rating and fires
  acidic webs that bypass Energy Shield.
- Unique Drop: Chitinous Eye + Weaver's Silk Cloak (+60 Evasion, +100% Poison Duration).
- World Shift ("The Venomous Bloom"):
  * Spiders worldwide gain +20% Evasion and stacking venom.
  * Unlocks Tier 2 Quality Station crafts for Evasion gear.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COLOSSUS 5: Aurum, the Gilded Gladiator (Act 2)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Location: Trial Chambers
- Spawn Ritual (The Vow of Poverty): Activate an Ominous Trial Spawner with
  Bad Omen V while having ZERO gold and ZERO armor equipped.
- Encounter: Moving spike walls and anvil traps. Aurum wields a tower shield
  that completely blocks frontal hits unless flanked or pierced.
- Unique Drop: Gilded Eye + Aegis of the Sun (Unique Shield: Blocks 100% elemental on timing).
- World Shift ("The Golden Age of Raids"):
  * Illager raids become Act 2 elite battalions with armor penetration.
  * Trial Chambers have a 25% chance to drop Regal Tomes & Tomes of Alchemy.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COLOSSUS 6: Ignis, Lord of the Molten Core (Act 2 CAPSTONE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Location: Bedrock Lava Lakes (Y <= -55)
- Spawn Ritual: Submerge into bedrock lava holding a Heart of the Sea. The lava
  solidifies into a Magma Glass arena as Ignis ascends.
- Encounter: Magma tidal waves that rapidly degrade armor durability and deal Fire/Phys.
- Unique Drop: Molten Core Eye + Eternal Infernal Igniter (ONLY tool that can light Nether Portals).
- World Shift ("The Gates of Hell Open - Act 3 Transition"):
  * NETHER PORTALS CAN NOW BE ACTIVATED.
  * Mobs globally scale to Act 3 stats.
  * Tier 7 Runes and Exalted Tomes begin dropping.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COLOSSUS 7: Charon, the Soul Quicksand Tyrant (Act 3)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Location: Nether Soul Sand Valley
- Spawn Ritual: Stand motionless on Soul Sand without boots until sinking and
  reaching 0 movement speed. Pulled into an underground soul crypt.
- Encounter: Gravity distortion, soul tornadoes, and mana drain.
- Unique Drop: Soulfire Eye + Charon's Ferryman Oar (50% Phys->Void, 10% Mana Leech).
- World Shift ("Soul Resonances"):
  * Soul Fire grants nearby players +25% Mana Recovery.
  * Nether Skeletons and Ghasts gain +40% Projectile Speed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COLOSSUS 8: Skarn, the Crimson Matriarch (Act 3)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Location: Bastion Remnant Treasure Room
- Spawn Ritual: Drop an Enchanted Golden Apple to a Piglin Brute and strike it
  while under the Potion of Weakness effect.
- Encounter: Giant armored boar-titan that charges through terrain, shredding 50% of player armor.
- Unique Drop: Crimson Eye + Tusk-Breaker Greatsword (2-Handed Colossus Sword with 40% Armor Pen).
- World Shift ("The Blood Frenzy"):
  * Piglins and Hoglins gain +25% Attack Speed and Bleed on hit.
  * Netherite forging yields double quality scaling.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COLOSSUS 9: Pyroth, the Blazing Sovereign (Act 3 CAPSTONE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Location: Nether Fortress
- Spawn Ritual: During a Nether Lava Storm, deflect a Ghast Fireball directly
  into a Blaze Spawner.
- Encounter: Aerial bullet-hell battle with concentric solar beams. Requires 50%+ Fire Res.
- Unique Drop: Solar Eye + Void Resonance Core (Unlocks Stronghold Portal Frames).
- World Shift ("The Void Beckons - Act 4 Transition"):
  * Stronghold End Portal frames can now receive the 12 Colossus Eyes.
  * All mobs globally scale to Act 4 (Endgame Tier).
  * T9-T10 Runes and Divine/Artificial Tomes begin dropping.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COLOSSUS 10: Astra, Sovereign of the Eclipse (Act 4)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Location: Mountain Peak (Y >= 220)
- Spawn Ritual: During a New Moon, stand on the highest peak with zero armor and
  stare into an Enderman's eyes for 10 seconds without looking away.
- Encounter: Upper-stratosphere starry arena with falling meteors and 90% Evasion phase-dodging.
- Unique Drop: Starlit Eye + Crown of the Eclipse (+150 ES, +50% Void Res, Void hits ES normally).
- World Shift ("Starlight Cascade"):
  * Energy Shield recharge rate increased by +30% globally.
  * Endermen deal pure Void damage.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COLOSSUS 11: Null, the Echoing Dread (Act 4)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Location: Ancient City Center
- Spawn Ritual: Trigger 5 Sculk Shriekers simultaneously while ringing a Bell at full volume.
- Encounter: Arena is pitch-black (Zero Visibility). Null is invisible and must be tracked
  by sound waves and footstep echoes.
- Unique Drop: Abyssal Echo Eye + Null-Blade Dagger (3.0x Crit multiplier from behind + Silence).
- World Shift ("Absolute Silence"):
  * Players deal +25% damage against Afflicted targets.
  * Sonic attacks gain 50% Armor Penetration.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COLOSSUS 12: Ouroboros, Keeper of the Threshold (FINAL COLOSSUS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Location: Stronghold End Portal Room
- Spawn Ritual: Stand on the End Portal frame carrying the 11 other Colossus Eyes
  while afflicted by ALL 5 Status Ailments simultaneously (Burn, Freeze, Shock, Poison, Bleed).
- Encounter: Time-weaving titan that cycles through all 4 elements (Fire -> Cold -> Lightning -> Void).
  Can rewind time to heal unless staggered by elemental weaknesses during transitions.
- Unique Drop: The Omega Eye (12th Eye) + World-Breaker Sigil.
- World Shift ("The End Opens"):
  * THE END DIMENSION PERMANENTLY UNLOCKS.
  * The path to the final Endgame is cleared!
