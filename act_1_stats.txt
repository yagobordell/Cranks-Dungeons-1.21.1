================================================================================
CRANKS DUNGEONS - ACT 1 VANILLA MOB STAT SYSTEM OVERVIEW
================================================================================

All vanilla mobs now have bespoke base stats, defenses, and elemental profiles
registered through com.cranks.dungeons.entity.MobStatRegistry.

These stats represent ACT 1 (Baseline). As the player slays the Capstone Colossi,
mob stats will scale proportionally in future Acts.
