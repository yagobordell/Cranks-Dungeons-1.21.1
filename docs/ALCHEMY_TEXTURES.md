# Alchemy texture sources

The final item PNGs live in `src/main/resources/assets/cranks-dungeons/textures/item/alchemy/`.
There are 32 reagent sprites and 18 bottle/body/overlay sprites, all 16 by 16 pixels.
The three resistance icons in `textures/mob_effect/` are 18 by 18 pixels, matching vanilla status icons.

Reagent artwork was generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool and exported to the native pixel grid with nearest-neighbor sampling and binary alpha cleanup. The regular bottle silhouettes and liquid masks use the Minecraft 1.21.1 potion, splash-potion, and lingering-potion textures. The lesser bottle bodies are now the mod author's own artwork, with matching liquid overlays described below. No enlarged or reinforced bottle variants are included. Failed mixtures use a subdued patterned fill and stable color. Potion colors are rendered through the potion component tint, rather than a separate image for every formula.

The resistance glyphs were extracted from the previously approved preview sheet. Final exports exclude the sheet's labels, background, and framing.

## Final reagent atlas prompt

Create ONE production sprite atlas containing exactly32 alchemy ingredient item sprites for Minecraft Java 1.21.1. Transparent background, no text, labels, lines, frames, shadows, checkerboard or decorations. Strictly eight columns and four rows of equal rectangular cells, center exactly one separate sprite in every cell. Each sprite is a native16x16 logical pixel Minecraft inventory icon, enlarged with nearest-neighbor crisp uniform square pixels. Use genuinely LOW resolution block shapes, at most16 pixels wide/high, palette 3-5 shades per material, like vanilla sugar, gunpowder, slime_ball, glowstone_dust and amethyst_shard. Flat item view, no perspective art, no gradients or antialiasing. Keep sprites isolated with generous transparent gutters for splitting the atlas. The32 sprites ordered left-to-right then top-to-bottom are: 1 red melon pulp with tiny green rind hint (pulp not a whole fruit);2 amber honey dollop;3 off-white bone powder;4 white sugar crystals;5 dark magma salt with subdued orange grains;6 crushed green turtle scute chips;7 beige rabbit-foot fibers;8 gold-orange carrot pulp;9 muted yellow pufferfish paste;10 pale blue phantom membrane flakes;11 dark maroon-brown fermented eye pulp;12 dark red spider eye pulp;13 tan-brown mushroom paste;14 deep lapis blue powder;15 pale turquoise wind essence inside a tiny plain glass ampoule, no glow or runes;16 tangled off-white cobweb fibers;17 green slime dollop;18 gray stone powder;19 icy pale-blue frost salt;20 muted copper-orange salt with one teal fleck;21 violet amethyst crystal cluster;22 violet amethyst powder;23 golden-orange glowing catalyst powder with a few purple grains and no external glow;24 ochre wheat suspension in a tiny plain gray-blue glass dish;25 pale green sugar-cane suspension in same dish;26 yellow dandelion suspension in dish;27 gray-blue clay suspension in dish;28 charcoal flint suspension in dish;29 chalk-white calcite suspension in dish;30 green slime suspension in dish;31 rusty-brown flesh suspension in dish;32 tan-brown leather suspension in dish. Last nine icons all use same humble shallow tiny glass dish silhouette, not a medieval metal bowl. No spoons. Every icon fits within a single16x16 logical pixel canvas and remains readable in a Minecraft inventory. This must look native to vanilla Minecraft, not an HD pixel art pack. Portrait/perspective/shading complexity is forbidden. Uniform orderly8x4 atlas, exactly32 cells.

## Verification

The exported sprites were inspected on a contact sheet and in the running Minecraft inventory. Model texture references and native pixel dimensions were audited. The alchemy screen uses the existing dark panel renderer, ornaments, text fields, and inventory dock.


## User-authored lesser bottles

The lesser drinking, throwable, and lingering bottle bodies were redrawn by the mod author. Their PNGs are preserved byte-for-byte. The corresponding normal and failed-mixture overlays now follow the enclosed interior of those shapes at 16 by 16 pixels, leaving the necks empty and retaining the author's highlights and outlines.

The shading was created with the built-in `image_gen` tool, then exported on the exact native pixel grid and clipped to the transparent glass interior. No generated bottle artwork replaced the author's files. The generated grayscale shades are normalized for in-game potion tinting; failed mixtures use a muted, mottled version of the same interior mask.
